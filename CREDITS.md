# Credits

The courtyard is built from Liberated Pixel Cup art, recolored to one warm dusty palette. Characters are layered from the Universal LPC Spritesheet Character Generator. The floor is one LPC flagstone family. The walls are LPC red brick with a sandstone coping. Chests, barrels, the lamp, and the chai awning are LPC base props.

Kite wears a cream kurta, brown waistcoat, and pyjama, with a small Gandhi cap drawn on the hair. Veil wears the Sara tunic and a saffron dupatta (the thin cloth headwrap plus the scarf). Her disguise is the tan tunic with the dupatta off. Key wears a cream kurta and a navy waistcoat. The constable's pagri is the cloth headwrap recolored red, with a short plume, khaki uniform, and the LPC cane as a lathi. The officer is the same khaki body with a pith helmet and red sash drawn on the down frame.

Original pixel props, drawn in `scripts/bake_lpc.py` at the same 32px size, are the pressure plate and its rune, the iron grate bars, the pebble, the stone well, the charpoy, the bicycle, the tonga, the kettle, the kulhad, the tulsi pot, the laundry, the jaali, the jharokha, the pillar, the curfew notice, and the Azad Hind poster. The poster is an original tricolour and tiger, not a copy of a historical painting or photograph. The notice reads "BY ORDER OF THE GOVERNOR / CURFEW". The wall graffiti reads "CHALO DILLI".
